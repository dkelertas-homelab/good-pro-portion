# Privacy policy addition: ads and consent

## Advertising

The free version of Nice Pro Portions shows a small banner ad on the home screen. Ads never appear during a workout. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
- approximate location (from your IP address)
- app interactions with ads (for example, views and taps)
- diagnostic data such as crash and performance information

This data goes to Google, who uses it to serve ads, measure them, prevent fraud and improve its services. It's encrypted in transit. We don't receive it or sell it, and the app itself stores no personal data. Google's practices are explained at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

### Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** the first time you open the app, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), which stops personalised ads.
- **Remove ads:** a one-off purchase in Settings turns off ads, and the ad SDK makes no ad requests after that.

### Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.
